# Pre-launch Product Decisions

El Roi Call does not have users yet. Treat current implementation as a prototype and optimize for learning before preserving legacy behavior.

## Keep

- El Roi Call name and Genesis 16 / El Roi foundation
- 1-855-619-SEES
- voice-first spiritual conversation
- matching a person's real situation to biblical stories
- The Well / recurring spiritual rhythm
- Gift a Call concept
- Cloud of Witnesses as the biblical content universe

## Change freely

- page structure
- visual layout
- copy
- call script
- provider stack
- membership packaging and price
- member dashboard
- onboarding
- data retention
- character/persona model
- analytics
- backend architecture

## Product hypotheses to validate before launch

1. One recognizable guide creates more trust than multiple biblical character voices.
2. Visitors connect faster when the first screen asks about their life rather than explaining the product.
3. People prefer story-led biblical reflection to AI roleplay.
4. A free first call is enough to test emotional usefulness before asking for membership.
5. Returning to the same guide can create a sustainable spiritual habit.
6. Raw call recording is not necessary for useful continuity if structured summaries are optional and user-controlled.
7. Pricing should be tested after the core call reliably creates value.

## Initial pilot measurements

- percentage who begin intake
- percentage who complete first call
- call duration distribution
- voluntary post-call rating
- "Did you feel understood?"
- "Did the biblical story feel relevant?"
- "Would you call again?"
- percentage who request another call
- safety escalation rate
- technical call failure rate

Do not optimize first for page views, time on site, or vanity engagement. Optimize for a completed, trusted, useful first conversation.
