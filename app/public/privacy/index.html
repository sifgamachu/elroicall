Privacy Policy

El Roi Call, a service of Teregna LLC · Updated September 7, 2026 · Pre-launch privacy policy

Pre-launch note. El Roi Call is being substantially rebuilt before public launch. This policy distinguishes the current prototype implementation from planned architecture. We will update this page before routing user data through a materially different provider or retention design. A design document is not a substitute for the behavior of the system actually processing your data.

1. What we may collect

  • Account and contact information: phone number, name if you share it, email, authentication data, and billing-related records if paid service is enabled. Full payment-card numbers are handled by the payment processor rather than stored by El Roi Call.
  • Conversation content: depending on the implementation active for a call, audio, transcripts, structured summaries, or other conversation state. These can contain sensitive information you voluntarily share, including grief, mental or physical health concerns, relationships, faith matters, or other personal circumstances.
  • Website inputs: intake text, prayer requests, gift notes, consent choices, and other information you type into forms.
  • Operational data: call times/durations, delivery status, provider/error logs, abuse-prevention signals, and similar technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide the experience you requested: recognize a free-call invitation, operate El Roi Guide, choose or retrieve relevant biblical content, maintain conversation state where permitted, schedule communications you requested, provide account/journey features, process billing if enabled, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and improve safety and quality. We do not sell the content of your conversations for advertising.

3. Current prototype service providers

The repository does not yet contain the complete production backend, so this list reflects the service providers identified by the current prototype implementation and prior production configuration: Anthropic (AI conversation), ElevenLabs (voice synthesis), Twilio (telephony), Deepgram (speech-to-text), Supabase (database/auth/backend services), Stripe (payments if enabled), and Cloudflare (website/edge infrastructure). Each provider may receive the information necessary for the function it performs.

The V2 architecture is being designed around Twilio plus OpenAI Realtime for the live guide, with fewer conversation-path providers. That migration is not treated as complete until the actual backend and telephony configuration have been changed and tested. This Privacy Policy will be updated before the changed provider stack is used for public calls.

4. AI disclosure

El Roi Guide is artificial intelligence. It does not impersonate Job, Esther, Hagar, Peter, or other biblical figures. Biblical figures are stories/witnesses the guide may discuss. The guide is not God, clergy, a therapist, or a medical professional.

5. Recording, transcription, and conversation memory

The current prototype may record and transcribe calls. The active call experience should disclose the processing that applies before the guided conversation continues. The V2 privacy target is to avoid permanent raw audio as the default product memory where feasible, prefer structured summaries, and give users meaningful control over remembered conversation context. Those target practices are not promised until implemented and verified in the backend.

6. Prayer requests

The Prayer Well form is designed not to request your name or phone number with the prayer text. Do not include identifying information in the prayer itself if anonymity matters to you. The product target is short-lived storage with automated deletion. Because the current database migration/RLS/TTL code is not yet present in this repository for verification, we are not making an absolute “destroyed forever” or verified-72-hour technical guarantee in this pre-launch version. The retention rule must be implemented, tested, and then stated precisely before launch.

7. Sensitive and consumer health data

Information you voluntarily share may reveal physical or mental health status, grief, anxiety, addiction, reproductive matters, disability, or other information that some laws treat as consumer health data or otherwise sensitive data.

Collection: sensitive intake forms should present the relevant AI/privacy disclosure before the text is submitted. Use: to provide the requested experience, safety, continuity where enabled, and necessary operations. Sale/advertising: El Roi Call does not sell conversation content for advertising.

You may contact privacy@elroicall.com regarding access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights that apply under applicable law. Before public launch, the final policy and consent design should be reviewed against laws applicable to the states/countries where the service will be offered.

8. Retention

Pre-launch retention must be minimized and documented per data type. The final system should distinguish account/contact data, consent records, billing records, raw audio (if any), transcripts (if any), structured summaries, prayer requests, gift data, and operational/security logs. No broad “keep everything while the account is active” default should be assumed for V2. Retention schedules become binding product promises only after the backend implementation and backup behavior are verified.

9. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the implementation, such as encryption in transit, access controls, provider secret stores, and least-privilege data policies. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach triggers notification obligations, we will provide notices as required by applicable law. Security claims on the site should match controls that can be demonstrated in code or provider configuration.

10. Children

El Roi Call is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly design the service for children. If we learn that we collected a minor's information contrary to the service rules, we will take appropriate deletion or protective steps.

11. Your choices

Depending on the feature and applicable law, choices may include declining sensitive intake, declining a gifted call, ending a call, declining prayer, opting out of recurring communications, disabling conversation memory where offered, and requesting access/deletion through privacy@elroicall.com.

12. Changes and contact

Because El Roi Call is pre-launch, this policy will change as the provider stack, storage model, and call architecture are finalized. The updated date will change with material revisions. Questions: Teregna LLC · El Roi Call · privacy@elroicall.com
