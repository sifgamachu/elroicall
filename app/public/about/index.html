Why this exists

Begin with the God who sees.

El Roi is the name Hagar gives God in Genesis 16 after being seen in a place where she had very little power and very few choices. El Roi Call starts there: not with an AI performance, but with the human need to be heard before someone starts giving answers.

“You are the God who sees me.”

Genesis 16:13

One guide

El Roi Guide is one recognizable AI voice. It listens, asks questions, opens Scripture, and can pray with the caller. The voice remains familiar even when the subject changes.

Many witnesses

Job, Hagar, Esther, Peter, Ruth, Elijah, Hannah, and the rest of Scripture are not synthetic characters. They are stories the guide can narrate and explore when their ground touches the caller's life.

Clear boundaries

The guide does not claim to be God, a biblical person, clergy, a therapist, or a crisis service. It does not claim private revelation or guaranteed outcomes from God. Trust matters more than immersion.

You do not have to understand the product first.

Start with whatever is actually happening in your life. The first conversation is where El Roi Call should prove whether the idea is useful.
